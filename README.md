# Quorum

### A home for your community, on your own terms.

*A self-hosted community platform for conversation, calls and connection — warm, fast and entirely yours.*

</div>

---

> **This is a showcase repository.** It contains no source code — only a tour of what Quorum is and the experience it creates.

## The idea

Great communities deserve a place of their own. Quorum gives you a beautiful, familiar space for your friends, team or fans to gather — hosted by you, shaped by you, and owned by you.

## What it feels like

- **Servers, channels and categories** to keep every conversation neatly in its place.
- **Direct messages and group chats** for the closer moments.
- **Crystal-clear audio and video calls**, in private chats or in shared channels.
- **Invites and roles** so welcoming people — and looking after them — is simple.
- **Reactions, pins and message search** to celebrate the good things and find them again.
- **Rich link previews** that bring shared content to life.
- **A built-in admin panel** with everything a host needs to keep the community running smoothly.

## Why you'll love hosting it

- **Yours, fully.** Self-hosted, so your community and its memories stay in your hands.
- **Light and quick.** Designed to run happily on modest hardware.
- **Ready in minutes.** A smooth first-run setup gets you from nothing to a thriving server fast.
