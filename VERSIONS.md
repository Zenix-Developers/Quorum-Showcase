# Quorum milestones

How Quorum grew, newest first. Back to the [overview](README.md).

## August 30, 2026: A calmer, sharper look

- A minimalist, Discord-style refresh of the whole app shell: a single accent color, three clear surface levels, and consistent shapes
- Navigation, sidebar, chat and composer tuned around an 8px rhythm
- A small, purposeful motion language for buttons, dialogs, drawers and new messages, with full reduced-motion support
- Light and dark themes polished side by side
- Unified dialogs, profile popovers and settings overlays

## August 28, 2026: Search that scales

- Full-text message search for direct chats, groups and server channels
- Search by message text, attachment filename and sender
- Quick results with paging, even on large histories

## August 27, 2026: Polish and accessibility

- Deeper accessibility: dialog focus handling, focus rings and keyboard-friendly controls
- Sturdier app state, so the interface stays smooth and consistent
- Refined UI details across chat, servers and settings

## August 27, 2026: Ready for the world

- Servers, channels, categories, direct messages and group chats
- Voice, video and screen-share calls with WebRTC and relay fallback
- Invites, roles and permissions, reactions, pins, link previews and uploads
- The admin panel, backups, and health and readiness checks

## August 10, 2026: Privacy by design

- Members-only attachments and careful channel access
- Safe link previews and per-account protections
